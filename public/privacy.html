Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dungeon Master ("the Bot", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects user data when operating on the Discord platform.

1. Data We Collect

To provide interactive RPG minigames, economy simulation, and Three Kingdoms strategy mechanics, we collect the following minimal data:

  • Discord User ID & Username: Used strictly to identify your player profile, save game progress, inventory, coins, and kingdom status.
  • Discord Guild (Server) ID: Used to associate server-specific settings, leaderboard rankings, and faction roles.
  • Command Usage & Game Events: Used to process game turns, combat resolutions, and anti-cheat validations.

2. How We Use Data

Your data is used solely for the functionality and enhancement of the Bot:

  • To manage player accounts, kingdom progression, and virtual inventories.
  • To display global leaderboards and server rankings.
  • To assign automated faction roles upon player request.

3. Data Storage & Security

All player data is stored securely in encrypted storage environments. We implement automated backups and strict access controls to ensure your game data is protected against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We NEVER sell, trade, rent, or share user data with third parties under any circumstances. All data remains exclusively within the Bot's operational infrastructure.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

We retain your game progress as long as you actively play. You have full ownership of your data and can request complete profile deletion at any time by contacting our support team or running !resetaccount.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or data deletion requests, please join our official Discord Support Server or contact the Developer Team.
